# KURDESTAN V3.9 — Rojava Evidence Layer

Date reviewed: 2026-09-06

## Scope
This release advances the 12 Rojava city records without inventing current population figures.

## Geographic verification
All 12 Rojava records now have a coordinate reference based on GeoNames. Qamishli and Hasakah also have elevation references in the source record.

## Population rule
Population is intentionally marked `not_currently_verified` for Rojava. Current city-level population must not be inferred from governorate totals, district totals, pre-war census values, or generic web estimates. Conflict, displacement and changing administrative conditions make the date and geographic level essential.

## Editorial rule
The site describes these records as geographic/cultural research profiles, not as political claims about sovereignty or administrative control. Ancient sites are not labelled as ethnically Kurdish without source support.
