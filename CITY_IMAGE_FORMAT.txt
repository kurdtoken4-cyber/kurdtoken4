Each city now has exactly ONE local placeholder image (generated SVG) under
assets/cities/<city-slug>.svg — no external image URL is used.

These are auto-generated placeholders (a colored card with the city name),
NOT real photographs — real, properly-licensed photographs were never
supplied for this project, and none were downloaded from the internet to
avoid using images without a license.

To finish the project, replace each assets/cities/<slug>.svg reference in
cities-data.js and assets/js/kurdtoken-101-cities-map.js with a real,
licensed photo of that city (jpg/png/webp all work — just update the path
in both files, since they are still two separate datasets).
