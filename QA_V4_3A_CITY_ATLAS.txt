KURDESTAN CITY ATLAS — V4.3-A DATA & EVIDENCE PACKAGE
Reviewed: 2026-09-07

Scope: 96 city dossiers across Rojhelat (32), Bakur (31), Bashur (21), and Rojava (12).

STATUS STANDARD
- city_level_verified: direct/primary, traceable city-level source in the dataset.
- city_level_verified_secondary_transcription: city-level value traceable through a secondary transcription of an identifiable official dataset.
- city_level_not_verified: no city-level figure is asserted as verified; province/district totals are not promoted.
- not_currently_verified: no reliable current city-level figure is asserted.

V4.3-A CHANGES
- Population status changes: 37
- All 32 Rojhelat 2016 city populations backed by the Statistical Center of Iran are marked city_level_verified.
- Bakur city-level figures with traceable city/urban source wording are marked secondary_traceable; district/province/metro totals remain unverified.
- Bashur secondary 2024 city values retain secondary_traceable status.
- Rojava remains research-needed for current city population; no figures were guessed.
- No population numbers were invented, rounded into existence, or replaced with district/province totals.

PRIMARY SOURCE CANDIDATES
- Iran: Statistical Center of Iran, 2016 Urban Population Census workbook.
- Türkiye: TÜİK ADNKS, latest 2025 results and historical city/district tables.
- Iraq: 2024 census/locality population tables; current dataset preserves secondary transcriptions where direct primary extraction is not yet embedded.
- Syria/Rojava: current city-level population remains research-needed due conflict/displacement and source comparability issues.

IMPORTANT
- population status is independent from overall dossier dataStatus. A city may have a verified population while other fields still require research.
- This package does not claim that all 96 dossiers are fully verified.
- Historical, cultural, craft, image, and other evidence fields remain governed by their individual evidence/source fields.
