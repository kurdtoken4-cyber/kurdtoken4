# KURDESTAN — Kurdish Clothing Research Standard

## Scope
This section documents Kurdish traditional dress (Cil û bergên Kurdî) as a diverse regional tradition rather than a single uniform costume. It covers women, men, everyday/ceremonial use, textiles, headwear, waist sashes, footwear, jewellery, and regional variation across Rojhelat, Bakur, Başûr and Rojava.

## Core sources consulted
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, “CLOTHING xvi. Kurdish clothing in Persia” (Peter Alford Andrews & Shirin Mohseni; updated 4 May 2017): regional variation in Iranian Kurdish dress; women’s and men’s garments; Mahabad, Quchan/Khorasan and other areas.
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, “CLOTHING xxv. Clothing of Khorasan”: detailed documentation of Kurdish clothing in northern Khorasan, including men’s headgear, shirts, jackets, trousers, overcoats and women’s garments.
- Kurdistan Regional Government Calendar 2026: 10 March is listed as National Clothing Day.
- Wikimedia Commons Kurdish clothing categories and individual files: openly licensed photographic/documentary examples with creator and licence information on the source pages.
- Kurdish Central clothing guide: terminology and component-level description for Central Kurdish/Sorani women’s dress.

## Editorial rules
1. Never present one regional style as the clothing of all Kurds.
2. Names of garments are preserved in Kurdish/transliteration and may differ by dialect.
3. Population, historical dates, and claims about symbolism are included only when sourced.
4. Photos are labelled with creator/licence/source where known; users should re-check the source page before redistribution.
5. “When worn” is separated into everyday, wedding, Newroz and cultural/ceremonial contexts because modern usage differs from historical usage.
6. The page distinguishes documented historical practice from contemporary revival/festival use.
