# KURDESTAN V4.1 — Bashûr City Dossier Completion

Reviewed: 2026-09-07

## Scope
- 21 Bashûr records retained; no city removed or silently merged.
- 96 total city records and 96 unique IDs retained.
- Added city-dossier fields for history, geography, language, culture, handicrafts, economy and heritage.
- Added 2024 city/locality population where a city-level transcription was located.
- Governorate/district populations remain separate and are never presented as city population.

## Population policy
City-level values marked `city_level_verified_secondary_transcription` are secondary transcriptions of the 2024 Iraq census/locality table. The site keeps the source type explicit rather than implying that the displayed page itself is the primary census table.

## Important distinction
KRSO reports the Kurdistan Region total at 6,519,129 in the 2024 census, with governorate density indicators for Erbil, Sulaymaniyah, Duhok and Halabja. Those regional figures are not used as city populations.

## Administrative values kept separately
The 2024 census also provides district/administrative-unit totals. Examples include Zakho district 398,876, Akre district 260,907, Kalar district 213,452, Amedi district 103,507, Koya district 110,172 and Chamchamal district 109,502. These are not inserted into city population fields.

## Disputed-area note
Kirkuk and Khanaqin are retained with neutral administrative wording. The site does not infer political status from the existence of a city record.
