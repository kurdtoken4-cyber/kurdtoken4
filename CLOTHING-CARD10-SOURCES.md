## Image policy
Images used in Card 10 are linked to Wikimedia Commons source pages or other source/licence pages. The page distinguishes historical evidence from contemporary festival clothing and avoids assigning a region to a photograph when the source does not establish the locality.

## Important research note
“Kurdish clothing” is not one uniform costume. Regional, tribal, urban, religious, class, age, gender and period differences are substantial. The catalogue therefore avoids presenting one style as representative of all Kurdish communities.
