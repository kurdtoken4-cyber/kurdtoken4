# KURDESTAN V4.7 — City Atlas Standard

V4.7 uses 11 evidence dimensions for every city dossier: identity, geography, population, history, heritage, culture, languages, economy, handicrafts, images, and sources.

## Population rule
Only city/urban-centre values with an identified year and traceable source may be presented as verified. Governorate/province/district totals are not silently converted into city totals.

## Research states
- **Documented:** 10–11 evidence dimensions documented.
- **Baseline:** 7–9 dimensions documented.
- **Partial:** fewer than 7 dimensions documented.

## Image rule
A city may have strong textual evidence while still requiring rights-safe/attributed imagery. Image evidence is therefore tracked independently.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer official statistics, UNESCO/other intergovernmental heritage records, academic sources, and recognized local institutions. Secondary transcriptions are labeled as secondary.
