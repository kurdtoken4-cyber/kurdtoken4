کەسایەتییە ناودارەکانی کورد

ئەرشیفێکی سەرچاوەدارە بۆ شاعیران، نووسەران، زانایان، مێژووناسان و کەسایەتییە سیاسی و بیرمەندە کوردەکان؛ لەگەڵ ژیاننامە، بەرهەم، سەرچاوە و وێنەی هەر کەسایەتییەک.

تێبینییەکی میتۆدۆلۆژی: ئەم ئەرشیفە کۆمەڵێکی فراوانی سەرچاوەدارە و دەتوانرێت دواتر فراوانتر بکرێت. بەروار، بەرهەم و ناساندنی کەسایەتییەکان بە وریایی و بە پشتبەستن بە سەرچاوە هاتوون.

Selection & balance

Cross-regional selection; major literary, scholarly, scientific, political and artistic contributions; women and diaspora represented; no claim of exhaustive totality.

Evidence levels

A = institutional/academic biography; B = major scholarly reference; C = specialist secondary source; D = provisional lead requiring further verification. Only A–C should be used for firm factual claims.

Identity & attribution

A birthplace, language or political affiliation does not automatically prove ethnic identity. Where scholarship differs, the card records the uncertainty instead of choosing a partisan formulation.

Image rights

Remote Wikimedia images are used only as sourced representations; each image should be checked for licence/attribution before production deployment. Pre-photography figures are never labelled as original photographs.

Scope boundary

Card 7 focuses on people. Card 5 contains general history; Card 8 focuses on music and musicians; Card 10 focuses on clothing; Card 13 governs research and evidence.
