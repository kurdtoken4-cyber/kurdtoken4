# KURDESTAN V5.54 — Stage 2B City Atlas Population Corrections

Date: 2026-09-15

## Scope
This stage corrects city-level population records in the Bashur/Iraq subset using the 2024 Iraq city/locality census table as transcribed by City Population. It does **not** substitute governorate totals for city populations.

## Corrected 2024 city/locality records
Erbil 1,001,219; Sulaymaniyah 828,850; Duhok 452,967; Kirkuk 1,249,197; Halabja 67,190; Zakho 398,876; Akre 68,093; Soran 179,596; Ranya 197,700; Kalar 145,164; Qaladiza 73,951; Koy Sanjaq 62,046; Chamchamal 65,274; Simele 71,557; Khanaqin 51,538.

## Important corrections
Several prior values were not consistent with the checked 2024 city/locality table. In particular, Halabja, Zakho, Akre, Soran, Ranya, Chamchamal and Simele were corrected rather than preserved merely for continuity.

## Unresolved Bashur records
Amedi, Rawanduz, Shaqlawa, Darbandikhan, Penjwin, Batifa and other records not present in the checked city/locality table remain explicitly research-needed. No governorate/district total is inserted as a city value.

## Source standard
Primary institutional population data are preferred. Where the official city/locality table is not directly exposed in the available source, the site records the value as a secondary transcription and keeps the evidence level explicit.

## Other regions
Iran 2016 city figures remain tied to the Statistical Center of Iran urban census workbook. Turkey city-level values are not replaced with province totals. Rojava/Syria values remain unverified where no traceable city-level census was found.
