# KURDESTAN V4.6 — Cultural Atlas & Research

V4.6 strengthens the 96-city atlas with a stricter evidence model, refreshed official source registry, population-status normalization, research coverage metadata and an in-site evidence legend.

This release is **not** a claim that every city field is fully verified. Missing evidence remains explicitly marked for further research.

## Key changes
- Population-status semantics normalized where a traceable city-level source exists.
- 2016 Iran city populations remain tied to the Statistical Center of Iran urban census workbook.
- Türkiye statistics point to TÜİK's 2025 ADNKS release.
- Kurdistan Region aggregate statistics point to KRSO's 2024 census indicators.
- UNESCO heritage references refreshed.
- Rojava population figures remain unguessed where current city-level evidence is insufficient.
- Research status legend added to the website.
