### Editorial rule
No single costume is presented as “the costume of all Kurds”. Every regional or historical statement is scoped to its evidence. Shared forms are not turned into exclusive ownership claims without direct evidence.

## V5.22 Re-audit additions — 2026-09-15

This re-audit strengthens the atlas rather than simply increasing record count. The card now explicitly separates: (1) garment/object description, (2) living practice, (3) regional attribution, (4) historical period, (5) visual evidence, (6) material provenance, and (7) safeguarding status.

### Research-grade controls
- A local garment term is not generalized to all Kurdish communities without evidence.
- Museum objects are not treated as automatic evidence of current living practice.
- Photographs are treated as contextual visual evidence, not automatic proof of date, provenance, ownership, or population-wide prevalence.
- Fibre/material provenance is separate from garment identity.
- Reconstruction/revival/stage costume must be labelled separately from historically documented dress.
- Contemporary photography requires consent/access conditions where applicable.
- Symbolic meanings of colours, motifs, jewellery, marital status, class, or religion are not inferred from appearance alone.
- Khorasan, Western Azerbaijan, Sanandaj and Kermanshah are retained as distinct micro-regional evidence modules because the reference literature documents meaningful differences.
