# KURDESTAN V4.4 — Data & Source Registry

## Purpose
V4.4 adds a visible source registry to the research section so visitors can distinguish source-backed material from editorial summaries and unresolved fields.

## Verification rules
- Keep the year and source with population figures whenever available.
- Never promote a governorate/province/district total to a city total without city-level evidence.
- Mark uncertain or unavailable fields as research-needed rather than filling them by inference.
- Prefer primary statistical institutions and UNESCO for heritage claims; use secondary transcriptions only when clearly labelled.
- Cultural and linguistic claims should distinguish scholarly evidence from contested interpretations.
