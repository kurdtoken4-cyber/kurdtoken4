# Card 11 — V5.15 Foodways & Food Atlas Master Audit

Final structural audit performed 2026-09-15.

- 48 atlas records retained and individually assigned a record type, status, rights note and last-reviewed date.
- Record titles now expose five-language labels while preserving canonical local names.
- Atlas schema now explicitly includes ID, name, region, community, ingredients, technique, season, occasion, evidence, source, rights, review, type and status.
- Added searchable atlas controls and evidence filtering.
- Source links are attached only where a traceable source is available; otherwise the record explicitly says field verification is required.
- A/B/C/D evidence is not silently upgraded.
- Shared-food rule remains active: no unsupported exclusive Kurdish attribution.
- Food families are distinguished from documented individual recipes.
- Image presence is never treated as proof of cultural attribution or licence.
- Five-language architecture is preserved; local food names/source wording remain canonical data.
- Version labels are unified to V5.15.
- No new factual claims were invented for unverified regions; gaps remain visible as research needs.
