## Evidence principles
1. A craft is documented as knowledge + skill + social context, not only as an object.
2. Archaeological objects are not automatically treated as proof of a modern ethnic identity.
3. Regional variation is retained; a city, village, tribe, workshop or diaspora practice is not generalized to all Kurds.
4. "Authentic" and "endangered" require explicit criteria/evidence.
5. Image search results and Pinterest are not accepted as licensing evidence.
6. Community terminology is retained as data, while historical-origin claims remain evidence-dependent.
