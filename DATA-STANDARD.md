# KURDESTAN data standard

1. Never invent population, area, dates or historical claims.
2. Every numeric city statistic should carry a year and source.
3. World Heritage facts use UNESCO pages as primary references.
4. Official regional statistics use the relevant national or regional statistical authority.
5. Historical claims that are contested are presented as interpretations with attribution, not as unquestioned facts.
6. Images loaded from Wikimedia Commons retain the file title, author and license metadata when available.
7. KURD Token contract facts are only published after on-chain deployment and verification.
