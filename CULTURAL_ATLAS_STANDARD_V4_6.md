# KURDESTAN Cultural Atlas Standard V4.6

## Scope
The atlas covers 96 city dossiers across Rojhelat, Bakur, Bashur and Rojava.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Primary official statistics / institutional registers.
2. UNESCO and other intergovernmental heritage records.
3. Official cultural/tourism institutions.
4. Reputable secondary transcriptions, clearly labeled.
5. General databases only for coordinates or discovery unless independently corroborated.

## Population rule
Population is city-level only. Province, governorate, district and regional totals must never be silently displayed as city populations. A figure requires a year and source. Secondary transcriptions remain labeled as secondary until checked against a primary table.

## Cultural claims
Language, identity, customs, crafts and historical claims must be written without unsupported exclusivity. Where scholarship or sources disagree, the site should label the disagreement rather than presenting one contested interpretation as settled fact.

## Images
Every image should have an alt description and a traceable source/license. If rights status is unclear, use a local fallback or a rights-cleared replacement rather than silently presenting an unlicensed asset as archival evidence.

## Editorial status
`verified` = direct/traceable evidence; `documented` = evidence exists but dossier is incomplete; `research-needed` = evidence is insufficient and the missing field is not guessed.
