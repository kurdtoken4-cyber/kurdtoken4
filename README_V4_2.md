# KURDESTAN V4.2 — ALL-CITY DOSSIER STANDARD

This release applies one evidence standard to all 96 city records across the four project regions.

## Coverage
- Rojhelat: 32 records
- Bakur: 31 records
- Bashur: 21 records
- Rojava: 12 records
- Total: 96 records / 96 unique IDs

## Accuracy rule
No province, governorate, district or metropolitan total is silently promoted to city population. Every population value must carry a year and source. Missing city-level values remain unverified rather than being guessed.

## 11 evidence dimensions
Identity, geography, population, history, heritage, culture, languages, economy, crafts, images, sources.

Each record now includes a city-level research plan, regional primary-source candidates, data-quality rules and the review date 2026-09-07.

## Key primary-source anchors
- Iran: Statistical Center of Iran / 2016 urban census workbook.
- Türkiye: TÜİK / ADNKS 2025.
- Kurdistan Region of Iraq: KRSO / 2024 census population indicators.
- Syria: Central Bureau of Statistics Syria; city-level values require dated, traceable evidence.
- Heritage: UNESCO World Heritage Centre where applicable.

## Important limitation
“Complete dossier structure” does not mean every factual field is verified. A field marked research-needed is intentionally left open to prevent invented or weakly sourced claims.
